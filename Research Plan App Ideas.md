# **Title**

Research Plan App

# Purpose

Help product team members, researchers, and non-researchers structure a research plan by providing guidance and feedback.

# Problem

Repetitive task that I have found myself spending considerably amount of time or task that no user receive feedback and go ahead and do it by themselves. 

# Task

**Writing a conscious research plan.**

By *conscious*, this means a plan that encourages people to think carefully about:

  - The research
  - The project
  - The plan itself
  - The overall impact

# Context

Writing a research plan is an essential task for research and is the most consequential step in a research project.

  - **Impact on success:** If done correctly, it sets the stage for a successful project. If done incorrectly, later improvements may not have a significant impact.
  - **Team alignment:** It plays a key role in aligning product teams.
  - **The challenge:** Writing a plan is a difficult task—both for people who are not trained in research and even for those who are.
  - **Common feedback patterns:** Experienced researchers often find themselves providing similar, repetitive feedback to help colleagues improve their plans.
  - **The solution:** Given the movement toward research democratization and the need to move quickly, a tool that automates feedback and supports learning can be invaluable for product teams.

# Benefits

  - **Higher quality:** Improves the overall quality of research plans.
  - **Skill building:** Upskills colleagues across the organization.
  - **Greater impact:** Leads to a higher impact from research efforts.
  - **Resource optimization:** Enables better, more efficient use of research resources in a company.

# Principles for Development

  - **Zone of Proximal Development:** Provide the right level of feedback tailored to the user's current skill level.
  - **Encouragement over discouragement:** Motivate people to write plans rather than making them feel inadequate about their skills.
  - **Focus on learning:** Emphasize continuous improvement, promote active thinking, and base guidance on solid information.
  - **Simplicity:** Keep the user experience straightforward and easy to navigate.
