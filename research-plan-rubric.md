# Problem

- Clarity: Names the affected user segment and the context they're in
- Specificity: Includes a quantifiable metric (%, $, count, rate)
- Feasibility: Scope is narrow enough to be addressed by one research effort
- Significance: States or implies the business/user impact of solving it
